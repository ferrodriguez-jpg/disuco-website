Our Story

Founded in 2017, Disuco set out to transform the logistics landscape, starting with pioneering last-mile delivery solutions. Our journey from a modest logistics provider to a powerhouse integrator and digital consultancy highlights our commitment to innovation and customer satisfaction.

4PLSolutions

We've redefined logistics by introducing Latin America's first Euro 6 BRT transport trucks and developing type 4PL solutions for the Oil & Gas industry. These steps were just the beginning.

The launch of Aduanapp in 2023 marked a strategic pivot to specialize further in customs and international transport, allowing us to enhance our core offerings in transportation and insurance.

200

As a non-asset company with a robust network, Disuco connects over 200 units across the USA and Mexico, delivering diverse, effective supply chain solutions. We pride ourselves on superior service and leveraging the strengths of our partners to ensure every client receives the best possible outcome.
